# BC harvesting-authority source access

The Province of British Columbia catalogue assigns the **Open Government Licence – British Columbia** to Forest Tenure Harvesting Authority Polygons. The licence status is not the blocker.

As checked on 2026-08-14, the catalogue lists a BCGW Custom Download distribution without an artifact URL. The official MapServer is a mutable service with 46,833 current features, a 1,000-feature response cap, and no publisher edition/version marker. It is useful discovery evidence, not a reproducible raw snapshot.

No file was downloaded, staged, transformed, ingested, or promoted. The recorded access-request draft asks the data steward for a complete, versioned/as-of custom export with count, schema, CRS, checksum, and attribution. It is deliberately unsent.

Harvesting-authority polygons identify a legally cleared authority area, not completed harvest. `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, and `RETIRED` status values must not be normalized as completion without a separately reviewed lifecycle mapping.
