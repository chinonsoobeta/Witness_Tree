## Candidate entries after normal source-ledger work

The following may be evaluated for a future source-ledger entry only after their specific resource URL, publisher/custodian, effective and retrieval date, spatial and temporal coverage, licence/attribution, immutable retrieval checksum and transform version are recorded:

- Alberta AVI Crown.
- Ontario FRI Term 2.
- Ontario Harvest Area, as tabular context only.
- Québec MRNF historical `Feux de forêt` data under the dataset's CC BY 4.0 terms.
- NRCan Annual High-Resolution Forest Land Cover for its published 1984–2022 period only.

Candidate is not ingestion approval. It does not grant rights beyond the verified catalogue licence, and does not turn a historical or tabular resource into a live/perimeter or spatial-record source.

## Explicit blocks

- Alberta Post-Inventory Final Cutblocks and PLVI lack a verified current authoritative source record.
- Ontario FRI Term 1 lacks a verified current authoritative source record.
- Current BC, Alberta and Ontario wildfire automation remains blocked until the precise service/item ID, resource metadata, cadence and applicable reuse terms are captured.
- SOPFEU live reuse remains blocked until SOPFEU gives written permission; its website terms are not an open-data licence.
- Elections Canada 45th-boundary and CPCAD use remain blocked until the applicable terms, attribution and selected release are captured.

The Québec MRNF historical CC BY wildfire dataset and SOPFEU live information must never be treated as the same source or as carrying the same reuse rights.
