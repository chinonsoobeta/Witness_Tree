# Phase 1 access-blocker resolution

On 2026-08-14, every one of the 13 canonical `access-blocked` production rows was rechecked against its named primary official source. Mailbox evidence through 2026-08-21 records eight substantive replies touching eight unique rows. The newest confirms receipt of the FOM-only copyright form, asks how view-only data would be used, and received an owner-authorized reply that rejects scraping, bypass and redistribution and requests a Province-authorized access path. It supplies no permission or access. No reply supplied an authorized artifact or resolved a rights, authority, precision, snapshot or engagement gate. The row-level reply state is in [`data/phase1-outreach-reply-audit.json`](../data/phase1-outreach-reply-audit.json).

No row was acquired. That is an intentional result, not an absence of work: none has both a coherent reusable artifact (or transaction-safe API) and the source-specific rights, authority, or engagement evidence needed by the Phase 1 ledger. Downloading a public service under an Access Only licence, stitching non-transaction-safe pages, treating a web explorer as a released package, or digitizing an approximate treaty map would create false evidence.

The first two rows are the highest-impact practical resolution paths. BC's FTA cutblocks and harvesting-authority records have OGL-BC reuse terms but lack a publisher-bounded coherent export. Their named data steward can issue BCGW Custom Download exports with the as-of/version, feature count, CRS, schema, checksum and attribution needed to begin normal acquisition. This repository does not submit that request or treat a live paged service as its replacement.

The BEC v13.1 route was inspected read-only on 2026-08-20. The official catalogue record identifies the publisher, version 13.1 (2026-07-08), EPSG:3005 resource and OGL-BC, but marks the resource as indirect access with no file URL. The publisher's distribution workflow requires order parameters, a notification email, a Terms and Conditions acceptance, submission and confirmation; the BCGW access policy further requires an eligible Province employee, contractor, agent or representative account. No order, acceptance, download or payment was made. The complete route audit and prepared owner action are in [`data/phase1-bec-custom-download-route-audit.json`](../data/phase1-bec-custom-download-route-audit.json); the row remains blocked.

The official public alternatives were also exhausted without a new order or terms acceptance. The public WFS reports 17,870 features but caps one response at 10,000, declares paging non-transaction-safe and exposes no feature version; the two official ArcGIS query layers report 15,666 and 17,870 records respectively, support paging, and expose no versioned snapshot or checksum. WMS is display-only, the catalogue KML loader returned 404, and the direct PDFs are cartographic/revision reports. A small 74,839-byte OGL-BC hectare-summary workbook was lawfully acquired and checksum/integrity checked, but contains no geometry or vector feature count. The machine-checked exhaustion and exact unsent owner action are in [`data/phase1-bec-public-alternative-exhaustion.json`](../data/phase1-bec-public-alternative-exhaustion.json); it adds zero raw credit and does not change the BEC, VRI, FOM or TAP blockers.

Rows 3–9 require a publisher artifact and/or written reuse decision. Rows 10–13 additionally require authority and Indigenous engagement/right-of-reply decisions. The federal historic- and modern-treaty candidates are specifically unsuitable for legal or precision geometry, independent of whether a map can be viewed or downloaded.

Run `npm run check:phase1-access-blocker-resolution`. It rejects missing rows, a non-primary HTTPS citation, a missing underlying evidence record, rank drift, an altered ledger state, or any claim that a blocked row is lawfully acquirable now.
