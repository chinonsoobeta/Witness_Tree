# CWFIS National Burned Area Composite access

The plan’s `cwfis-historical` row combines the National Burned Area Composite (NBAC) and Canadian National Fire Database context. NFDB polygons are separately staged evidence, but they do not replace NBAC.

CWFIS provides NBAC through its Datamart request route under an end-user agreement. The checked agreement is not an open-data licence: it grants internal-use rights and does not verify the public redistribution, sublicensing, immutable-archive, transformation, or public derivative-output rights required by this project. NBAC therefore must not be downloaded or treated as staged, immutable, transformed, ingested, or production eligible.

`data/cwfis-nbac-access-block.json` records the exact official request route and an unsent bilingual authorization request. Written authorization must identify the permitted edition/artifact, coverage, cadence, checksum/version identifiers, archive and transformation rights, public redistribution/sublicensing terms, attribution, and correction contact before any acquisition.
