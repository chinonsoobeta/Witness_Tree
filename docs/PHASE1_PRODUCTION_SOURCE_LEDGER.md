# Canonical Phase 1 production-source ledger

[`data/phase1-production-source-ledger.json`](../data/phase1-production-source-ledger.json) is the canonical reconciliation of the 31 production rows in the authoritative Phase 1 inventory. It is deliberately separate from [`data/source-ledger.json`](../data/source-ledger.json), which remains an illustrative fixture and must never be mistaken for acquired data.

The ledger has no production-eligible source. Four remote-evidence rows have a separately recorded, source-ledger-only owner decision. The four current-wildfire rows now have owner-approved geometry/operational scope and conditional transformation, ingestion, release and production admission; their six-object primary readback gate is integrated, but none can activate until any required recovery/mutation provenance and separate downstream decisions are proven. The ledger records the exact current evidence state and forces every row to name all required proofs: licence, attribution, retrieval/version, checksum, raw archive or official re-fetch path, profile, immutable archive, and a separate production-admission decision. It fails closed if a row is omitted, a local/profiled record is given immutable credit, raw credit is inflated, evidence references do not exist, or production is inferred from archive/profile evidence or owner approval alone.

Current evidence-state counts are: 7 remote-verified/archive/profiled, 9 local-verified/profiled, 2 partial components, and 13 access-blocked rows. This is 31 rows; none is unaddressed. The raw-evidence numerator is **14.25/31**. The fixed 25-point baseline plus the 30-point raw-evidence component computes a formal evidence-tracking score of **38.7903226%** (`25 + 30 × 14.25/31`, rounded to seven decimal places). The complete-ledger portion remains 0/31, therefore the separate 45% production-ledger component contributes **0 percentage points**. This score is not source-ledger admission, transformation, analysis, ingestion, public release, production admission, or production eligibility. The NRCan harvest and canopy-height rows now have redacted exact primary/recovery archive evidence, but their owner source-ledger decisions remain pending. The Québec fourth-inventory row is local verified/profiled only: its immutable archive, transformation, ingestion, admission, and eligibility fields remain false. Coverage-geometry completion does not change the production-ledger component. The Alberta PLVI row is remotely archived only: its record explicitly excludes owner source-ledger admission, transformation, analysis, ingestion, public release, production admission, and production eligibility. Every current access-blocked row has a primary-source, fail-closed resolution record in [`PHASE1_ACCESS_BLOCKER_RESOLUTION.md`](PHASE1_ACCESS_BLOCKER_RESOLUTION.md); the record supplies no source credit or admission.
