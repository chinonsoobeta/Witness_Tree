# Canonical Phase 1 production-source ledger

[`data/phase1-production-source-ledger.json`](../data/phase1-production-source-ledger.json) is the canonical reconciliation of the 31 production rows in the authoritative Phase 1 inventory. It is deliberately separate from [`data/source-ledger.json`](../data/source-ledger.json), which remains an illustrative fixture and must never be mistaken for acquired data.

The ledger has no production-admitted source. Four remote-evidence rows have a separately recorded, source-ledger-only owner decision; none authorizes transformation, ingestion, release, runtime production eligibility, or any other source. The ledger records the exact current evidence state and forces every row to name all required proofs: licence, attribution, retrieval/version, checksum, raw archive or official re-fetch path, profile, immutable archive, and a separate production-admission decision. It fails closed if a row is omitted, a local/profiled record is given immutable credit, raw credit is inflated, evidence references do not exist, or production is inferred from archive/profile evidence.

Current evidence-state counts are: 5 remote-verified/archive/profiled, 11 local-verified/profiled, 2 partial components, and 13 access-blocked rows. This is 31 rows; none is unaddressed. The raw-evidence numerator is **13.75/31**. The fixed 25-point baseline plus the 30-point raw-evidence component computes a formal evidence-tracking score of **38.3064516%** (`25 + 30 × 13.75/31`, rounded to seven decimal places). The complete-ledger portion remains 0/31, therefore the separate 45% production-ledger component contributes **0 percentage points**. This score is not source-ledger admission, transformation, analysis, ingestion, public release, production admission, or production eligibility. The Québec fourth-inventory row is local verified/profiled only: its immutable archive, transformation, ingestion, admission, and eligibility fields remain false. Coverage-geometry completion does not change the production-ledger component. The Alberta PLVI row is remotely archived only: its record explicitly excludes owner source-ledger admission, transformation, analysis, ingestion, public release, production admission, and production eligibility. Every current access-blocked row has a primary-source, fail-closed resolution record in [`PHASE1_ACCESS_BLOCKER_RESOLUTION.md`](PHASE1_ACCESS_BLOCKER_RESOLUTION.md); the record supplies no source credit or admission.

[`data/phase1-source-ledger-decision-readiness.json`](../data/phase1-source-ledger-decision-readiness.json) is the complementary non-admitting audit. It gives a complete, machine-checked answer to the next owner-decision question without changing any ledger field: 4 decisions are recorded, 7 rows need immutable archive evidence before an ordinary named source-ledger decision, 5 additionally need an operational or geometry-scope decision after archive, and 15 remain external-evidence blocked. The Elections Canada 2025 SHP payload is one physical artifact shared by two required ledger rows, so it has one archival proof and two named row decisions—never duplicate uploads or duplicate immutable objects.
