# Phase 1 Indigenous-boundary source gate

Status: **blocked discovery only**. No geometry was downloaded, staged, profiled, transformed, archived, ingested, or used in a map or analysis by this gate.

## Reserve and legislative-land boundaries

The primary federal record found is NRCan's [Aboriginal Lands of Canada Legislative Boundaries](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/522b07b9-78e2-4819-b736-ad9208eb1067). It describes a monthly-maintained vector service for Indian Reserves, selected land-claim settlement lands, and Indian Lands. Its catalogue lists an official REST service and downloadable SHP resources, but says the downloadable resource is the former GeoBase product and its attributes differ from the service while alignment remains in progress.

This is not an ISC release. No Indigenous Services Canada authored, authoritative reserve-boundary download was verified. Do not substitute NRCan's record as proof of ISC provenance. No raw artifact may be acquired until the owner records the authoritative edition to use, licence/reuse and redistribution permission, required attribution, release/version, and the Indigenous engagement/right-of-reply path.

## CIRNAC finalized modern-treaty map

CIRNAC's [Modern treaties](https://www.rcaanc-cirnac.gc.ca/eng/1677073191939/1677073214344) page describes a map of finalized modern-treaty boundaries. Its own disclaimer controls this gate: the map is for informal purposes; boundaries are approximate and may be revised; and the drawings must not be relied upon for any purpose or activity, including assessment of consultation obligations. No CIRNAC vector endpoint, authoritative precision geometry, licence, or redistribution permission was verified.

Accordingly this map is discovery/contact context only. It is excluded from spatial joins, area calculations, overlays, ranking, legal/consultation inference, and all precision analytics.

## Fail-closed admission requirements

An acquisition proposal must separately prove all of the following before any raw download:

1. A primary federal publisher and exact authoritative release/version.
2. Written reuse, redistribution, and attribution terms for the selected artifact.
3. A source-specific scope and precision statement that supports the proposed use.
4. A named Indigenous engagement and right-of-reply route for every affected geography.
5. A checksum-bound, dated raw-artifact record after authorized retrieval, with no production or ingestion claim before independent review.

Historic treaty illustrations are explicitly out of scope for precision analytics. This gate does not identify Indigenous territories, rights, title, or relationships.
