# External gates

The implementation plan contains decisions that software cannot honestly manufacture. They remain external or release gates until authoritative evidence is recorded. This matrix reconciles the plan's Phase 0, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 checkpoints with the related ongoing source and wildfire gates. A checked-in document, fixture, automated test, or technical-preview deployment is not evidence that one of these gates is closed.

Status wording is deliberately conservative: **not recorded**, **not started**, **not requested**, **not confirmed**, and **required** do not imply progress or approval.

## Interpretation

These rows are launch constraints, not a backlog. Work may continue on illustrative fixtures, technical contracts, source discovery, or controlled acquisition without advancing an external-gate status. A status may change only when the corresponding authoritative evidence is recorded and reviewable.
