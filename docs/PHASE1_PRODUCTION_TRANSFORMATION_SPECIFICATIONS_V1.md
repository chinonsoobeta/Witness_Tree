# Phase 1 production transformation specifications (scope approved; not executed)

[`data/phase1-production-transformation-specifications-v1.json`](../data/phase1-production-transformation-specifications-v1.json) supplies five checksum-bound proposed transformations: annual NTEMS land cover, NTEMS harvest year, canopy cover, canopy height, and the two federal electoral ledger rows from one 2023 Representation Order archive. It binds only existing staged/archive/profile evidence and deliberately sets every future output SHA-256 to `null`.

Each specification fixes inputs, selected values or fields, CRS/grid/geometry policy, a non-overwriting derived path, sidecar fields, exclusions, QA gates, intended use, and prohibited claims. The annual product preserves every published VLCE2 class; it does not select a forest crosswalk. Harvest retains the publisher’s mapped year classes and is not an operational cutblock record. Canopy products preserve their continuous Float32 samples without thresholding. The federal output preserves 352 source polygons rather than dissolving multipart districts; `FED_NUM` plus `REPORDER` is the identifier, not district names.

The five specifications in this file, together with the two Québec stand-copy specifications under [`data/transformation-specs/`](../data/transformation-specs/), are covered by the separate owner approval record [`data/phase1-transformation-scope-owner-approval-2026-08-25.json`](../data/phase1-transformation-scope-owner-approval-2026-08-25.json). That record approves the seven named transformation designs only. It does not authorize execution, ingestion, release, production admission, production eligibility, or external mutation. The specification files remain non-admitting and retain null/unexecuted output state; any execution must create new checksum-bound artifacts, and ingestion, release, and production admission remain separate owner decisions.

Run `node scripts/check-phase1-production-transformation-specifications-v1.mjs` to verify that the input evidence hashes still match and that no pre-execution output checksum is claimed. Run `node scripts/check-phase1-transformation-scope-owner-approval.mjs` to verify the separate seven-scope decision and its packet/specification bindings.
