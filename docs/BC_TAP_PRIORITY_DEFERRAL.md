# BC TAP priority-deferral current-view block

This record addresses the old-growth half of the Phase 1 `bc-old-growth-bec` inventory row. It is separate from, and cannot be fulfilled by, the BEC ecosystem-map record.

The official [BC Data Catalogue record](https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/f257ca4a-0c33-4eb2-9da8-21dff4482f58) identifies **Old Growth Technical Advisory Panel (TAP) - Priority Deferral Areas - Current View**. Its live official ArcGIS layer is [MapServer/69](https://delivery.maps.gov.bc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/whse/bcgw_pub_whse_forest_vegetation/MapServer/69), declares EPSG:3005, reports 255,486 records on 2026-08-14, and has a maximum record count of 1,000.

No body was acquired. The catalogue assigns **Access Only**, rather than an open-data licence. The Province's linked copyright terms say Access Only catalogue material may not be reproduced or redistributed without prior written permission. The record also says this TAP current view **does not represent the supported old-growth deferral areas currently being implemented**. A live service with pagination is not a versioned immutable artifact; no publisher checksum or consistent-snapshot statement was identified.

Consequently it is blocked from archival, staging, transformation, ingestion, and production use. It is also not evidence of a legal protected area, a harvest prohibition, or a currently implemented deferral.
